**2. Diagnosis reasoning:**
- `harness-config.md` scoped me to `receipts/` and gave the verify command.
- `prompt.md` said `--tz America/New_York` gives wrong totals near a month boundary, and named the failing test.
- Reading `receipts/grouping.py`, the function signature `group_expenses_by_month(rows, tz: str)` takes a `tz` argument, and the module's own docstring spells out the required behavior: "a receipt whose UTC timestamp crosses a month boundary once converted to `tz` groups into the local month, not the UTC one." But the implementation never referenced `tz` at all — it parsed `ts` (a UTC-aware datetime, since `_parse_row` does `fromisoformat(...replace("Z","+00:00"))`) and grouped directly via `ts.strftime("%Y-%m")` on the UTC value. That's a straightforward case of an accepted-but-unused parameter: the timezone conversion step was simply missing, so any receipt near midnight UTC at a month boundary got bucketed by the UTC month instead of the local one. The fix converts the UTC-aware `ts` into the target zone via `astimezone(zone)` before deriving the month key.
